Overview

Predict is an iPhone app plus a Google Cloud Run service. The phone stores your settings locally. When you are signed in, Cloud Run also stores a copy of those settings on your user in Firestore so Auto-trade, Home Buy / Sell, and Protect money use the same rules as Settings.

What we collect

  • On device: Kalshi key ID & PEM (iOS Secure Store / Keychain), app settings, trade/alert history.
  • Cloud (your user in Firestore): Settings you choose in the app, including Shared Risk limits (max open, trades/day, 15-minute window, daily loss stop), Home Buy tab values (size, minutes, max ask, time in force, chase), Auto-trade tab values (the same size/timing fields plus Protect money), cushions, asset on/off, Auto-trade On/Off, and related flags. Trade and alert records that Cloud Run writes for fills, skips, and settlements.
  • Cloud (Secret Manager): If you save Kalshi API credentials, Cloud Run stores the key so it can place Auto-trade, Home Buy / Sell, and Protect money orders. The phone never talks to Kalshi.
  • Subscriptions: Apple and RevenueCat process purchase receipts and entitlement status associated with your Apple ID. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Support email: If you contact us, we receive the email content you send.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers
  • No sale of personal data
  • We do not hold your Kalshi cash. Money stays in your Kalshi account.

Third parties

Kalshi (market data & orders), Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Firestore, Secret Manager), Apple (App Store / subscriptions), and RevenueCat (entitlement validation). Their policies apply to their processing. Predict does not control Kalshi order outcomes or guarantee profitable results from any data shown in the app.

Data retention

Local app data remains until you delete it in-app or uninstall. Cloud user documents, trades, and alerts are retained until you delete the account or they age out of server purge settings. Subscription records are retained by Apple/RevenueCat per their policies.
