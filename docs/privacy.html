Overview

Predict stores your Kalshi API credentials on-device using the iOS Secure Store / Keychain. Trading history and alerts are stored locally on your device.

What we collect

  • On device: Kalshi key ID & PEM, app settings, trade/alert history.
  • Subscriptions: Apple and RevenueCat process purchase receipts and entitlement status associated with your Apple ID. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Support email: If you contact us, we receive the email content you send.

What we do not do

  • No advertising trackers
  • No sale of personal data
  • Kalshi private keys are not uploaded to Predict servers (there is no Predict trading backend)

Third parties

Kalshi (market data & orders), Apple (App Store / subscriptions), and RevenueCat (entitlement validation). Their policies apply to their processing. Predict does not control Kalshi order outcomes or guarantee profitable results from any data shown in the app.

Data retention

Local app data remains until you delete it in-app or uninstall. Subscription records are retained by Apple/RevenueCat per their policies.
