How Predict works

Think of each coin as a new 15-minute ticket: “Will Gold be above this line when the clock ends?” Yes costs some cents. No costs the rest. One side pays $1. The other pays $0.

You turn assets On in Cushions. That tab also sets a dollar gap — how far live price should be from the line before a cushion path may buy. Special paths (Last-minute, Spike fade, Pair lock, TWAP) often ignore that dollar gap and only check the On/Off chip.

The iPhone never talks to Kalshi. Cloud Run does. Home is a tap. Auto paths run on a timer. Most buys are IOC: fill now or cancel. A miss is not a fill and does not use the day cap.

Simple picture. Gold 3:00–3:15. Live gold is $20 above the line. If your Gold cushion is $15, Auto may buy YES. If the gap is only $2, Auto sits out. Last-minute might still try near 3:14 if that path is On and nobody already owns the ticket.

Shared limits

These four knobs sit on Settings → Risk → Shared. They apply to Home and every Auto path. They do not care which path you like.

Max open positionsDefault 5. How many unfinished tickets you may hold at once. Set 1 if you want one thing at a time.
Max trades / dayDefault 100. Counts filled buys only. A later sell of that fill is not a second trade. IOC misses do not count. Extra Last-minute clips, Step buy lots, and a Pair lock hedge each count as their own filled buy.
Max trades / asset / 15m windowDefault 1. One new fill per coin per 15-minute contract for Home, Auto, Cash out, Gold fade, TWAP, Last-minute first clip, Step buy lot 1, Spike fade, and the Pair lock runner. Extra clips, extra lots, and the hedge are extra.
Daily loss stopDefault $50. If locked-in losses hit this number, new buys stop for the day.

Isolation

  • Does not use Cushions $ or asset chips
  • Window cap 1 is per coin, per 15 minutes

Limits

  • Day cap is all assets together
  • Raising window cap above 1 can stack the same coin

Risk

  • A high day cap + many extra lots can spend fast
  • Loss stop is a brake, not a refund
Tap path

Home Buy

You look at Last signals and tap Buy. Cloud places that order. Auto-trade can be Off. It is still real money.

Example. Gold shows YES, the gap is past your cushion, Home size is $5, ask is 62¢. You tap Buy. Cloud buys about 8 contracts. If Protect is On later, Protect may sell that Home fill. Home Sell is always IOC.
$ per trade / min / maxHow much cash this tap may spend. Default $5 / $1 / $5. Use Home tab, not Auto tab.
Minutes elapsed / leftDefault 2 and 2. Skip the noisy open and the last two minutes.
Max entry askDefault $0.90. Do not tap a very expensive ticket.
Time in forceDefault IOC. FOK = all or nothing. GTC can rest until the window ends.
Chase above askDefault 2¢. Tiny extra on the buy. Home Sell uses this as slippage.

Isolation

  • Does not use Smart buy
  • Will not buy a ticker Cash out, Gold fade, TWAP, Last-minute, Step buy, Spike fade, or Pair lock already holds
  • A Home fill uses window cap 1

Limits

  • You must tap
  • Cushions On + $ gap must pass

Risk

  • A tap spends even if Auto is Off
  • You can lose the full ask if it settles against you
Cushion path

Auto-trade

Cloud buys the lean when the dollar gap is big enough, on a schedule. This is the “the move already happened” path.

Example. WTI cushion $1.50. Live WTI is $2.10 above the line. Auto is On, $5, ask 58¢. Cloud buys YES. It will not chase the last minute (default minutes left = 2).
$ / min / maxAuto size. Separate from Home. Default $5.
Minutes elapsed / leftDefault 2 / 2. Auto sits out the last minute on purpose.
Max entry ask / TIF / chaseDefault $0.90, IOC, 2¢. Chase is also Protect sell slippage.
Smart buyDefault On. After cushion passes, also require Min extra chance (default 8¢). Home ignores this.

Isolation

  • Will not enter a ticker another path already holds
  • BTC/ETH leave Auto if TWAP lock is On
  • If you set minutes left to 0, Auto can collide with Last-minute — window cap 1 wins

Limits

  • Needs full Cushions $
  • If cushion already hit, you do not need Last-minute on that coin

Risk

  • A late, rich ask can still lose
  • Smart buy does not guarantee more wins
Exit only

Protect money

Does not buy. It can sell an Auto or Home fill if the gap grows enough after a wait. Default Off.

Example. You bought Gold YES. Cushion $15. Ratio 1.00×. After 45 seconds, if the gap is still $15 or more, Cloud sells IOC at the bid.
On / OffDefault Off. Auto-trade Off does not turn Protect Off.
Sell when gap ≥ cushion ×Default 1.00×. 1.5× waits for a bigger gap.
Wait after fillDefault 45s. So your own print does not dump you.

Isolation

  • Skips Cash out, Gold fade, TWAP, Last-minute, Step buy, Spike fade, Pair lock — those paths exit themselves

Limits

  • Home and Auto fills only

Risk

  • You may sell a ticket that would have paid $1
  • Bid slippage can be worse than the ask you paid
Partial cushion

Cash out

Buy when part of the cushion is already there, then try to sell into a richer bid. Admin must turn the block On first. Your switch stays Off until you turn it on. Empty chips = no Cash out buys.

Example. Gold cushion $175. Enter 60% = $105. Max ask 82¢, bid 88¢, stop 5¢. Gap hits $110, YES ask 80¢. Cloud buys. If the bid rises by the same edge you planned (or hits 88¢ if you paid 82¢), it sells. If the bid falls 5¢ below your fill, it sells.
Enter cushion %Default 60%. Not the full Cushions $.
Max ask / bidDefault $0.82 / $0.88. Bid must stay above max ask.
StopDefault 5¢ under fill. Range 3–10¢.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On = skip the buy, and sell if you already hold and the bid pile is thin.
AssetsMust also be On in Cushions. Uses Auto $ per trade.

Isolation

  • Checked coins use Cash out instead of normal Auto
  • Never shares an open ticker with Home, fade, TWAP, Last-minute, or Step buy
  • BTC/ETH leave Cash out while TWAP is On
  • Does not last-minute chase. Last-minute may still use an empty window

Limits

  • Minutes-left floor of 3 — never last minute
  • No Smart buy, no Protect, no Auto max ask

Risk

  • Partial cushion can still reverse
  • Stop can cut a full $0 settle, and also cut a $1 win
Gold only

Gold fade

When Gold is barely moving, buy the cheaper ticket and always get out. Not Cash out. Not Spike fade. Admin On first. Default Off.

Example. Gold gap is $2 (under Max gap $3). Cheap ask is 48¢. Cloud buys the cheap side. Take +6¢, stop −5¢, or flatten with 3 minutes left. Window end also dumps. A full cushion flip dumps on the way out.
Max gapDefault $3. Enter only when the dollar gap is this small or smaller.
Max cheap askDefault 50¢. The cheap ticket cannot be rich.
Take / stop / flattenDefault +6¢ / −5¢ / 3 minutes left.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On can skip or dump if the book is thin.

Isolation

  • Gold only
  • Does not share an open ticker with Home, Auto, Cash out, TWAP, Last-minute, or Step buy
  • Always dumps — no hold to $1

Limits

  • Ignores Cushions $ to enter
  • Uses Auto $ per trade

Risk

  • A quiet Gold can still run
  • Flatten and window-end sells can lock a loss
BTC / ETH

TWAP lock

Only BTC and ETH. Only when leftover seconds can still be $0 and Yes still wins. Then hold to $1. Most windows: no trade. Admin On first. Default Off.

Example. ETH has 40 seconds left. Banked price already covers the strike for a $0 leftover. Yes ask is 96¢ or less. Cloud buys Yes and holds to settlement. No stop. No dump.
AssetsBTC / ETH chips. Those assets must be On.
Max askDefault $0.96. Range 90–97¢.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On = fail closed (no buy) if book size is unknown or thin.

Isolation

  • Checked coins leave Cash out and Auto for the whole 15 minutes
  • Last-minute, Step buy, Spike fade, and Pair lock do not fight TWAP on those coins
  • Hold to $1

Limits

  • Yes only
  • $0 leftover lock only
  • Uses Auto $ per trade

Risk

  • If the lock was wrong, you can lose almost the full ask
  • No early exit
Late leftover

Last-minute

The cushion never showed. In the last seconds, buy the expensive favorite in small clips. Not a lock. Admin On first. Empty chips = no buys. Default Off.

Example. ETH had no Auto fill. With 80 seconds left, Yes is 88¢ and No is 12¢. Side = Both. Cloud buys Yes clips, then may add more every Ladder wait, until Max clips, Stop with ~10s, or a $1.00 ask. If flip is Off, it holds to settlement.
Entry askMost you will pay. Default in the high 80s–90s range.
SideYes, No, or Both. Both = expensive side only, not 50/50. Never Yes and No in the same window.
Both min favorite / gapBoth only fires when the favorite is rich enough and the two asks are far enough apart.
Watch start / First clip by / Stop withWhen quotes start, when the first clip may fire, when new clips stop (default about 10s left).
Ladder wait / Clip contracts / Max clipsPause between adds, size of each clip, max open clips.
Sell if flip ≥Default Off (0). If set, sell only on a real opposite-side flip that rich. Does not sell just because your fill looks worse.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On = skip the buy only.

Isolation

  • Does not pull coins off Cash out or Auto — those already sit out the last minute
  • Window cap 1 blocks the first clip if Home / Auto / Cash out / Pair lock runner already filled this coin
  • Later clips are extra
  • TWAP owns BTC/ETH if On
  • Open Step buy, Spike fade, or Pair lock sit this ticker out
  • If Last-minute is in its buy window and those paths have no lot/runner, Last-minute owns new buys

Limits

  • Ignores Cushions $
  • Size is Clip × live ask, not Auto $5
  • Protect and Home Sell do not exit these lots

Risk

  • Last seconds can flip
  • You can lose the full entry ask
  • Many clips = many day-cap fills
Add if thesis holds

Step buy

Buy a small lot after the window is underway, only if a slice of the cushion is still true. Add more only if the ask has not run away. Admin On first. Empty chips = no buys. Default Off.

Example. Gold, Start after 5 minutes, Cushion % 50%, lot 1 contract, Add wait 1 minute, Add band 2¢, Max lots 3, Stop 3¢. At minute 5 the lean is YES and 50% of the Gold cushion still holds. Cloud buys 1 YES. A minute later, if YES ask is the last fill or at most 2¢ richer, it adds. If the ask already jumped 8¢, it waits — it does not chase. If ask falls 3¢ under a lot’s fill, that lot sells. Lot 1 stop sells every remaining Step buy lot on that ticker.
Start afterDefault 5 minutes into the window.
Cushion %Default 50% of that coin’s Cushions $. Needed for lot 1 and every add.
Lot / Add wait / Add band / Max lotsDefault 1 contract, 1 min, 2¢, 3 lots. Band is lots 2+ only. Band 0 = next ask must match last fill.
Stop / Entry askDefault 3¢ under that lot’s fill / $0.80 max.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On = skip the buy. Stops still run.

Isolation

  • Follows Auto lean. Never both sides
  • Window cap 1 blocks lot 1 only
  • Open Step buy: Home / Auto / Cash out / Last-minute / Pair lock sit out
  • Last-minute owns new buys if it is in its window and Step buy has no lots yet
  • Protect skips these rows
  • Stop adding with 30s left (not a knob). Stops still run

Limits

  • Size is Lot × live ask
  • 5s grace after each fill
  • Sold lots free Max lots slots

Risk

  • Adding into a fading move stacks losses
  • Lot 1 stop dumps the whole stack
Fade the spike

Spike fade

When one side is very expensive and the other is very cheap, buy the cheap side and always dump. Not Gold fade. Gold fade stays Gold-only. Does not use Cushions $. Admin On first. Empty chips = no buys. Default Off.

Example. DOGE at minute 3. YES ask 78¢ (inside 75–80¢). NO ask 22¢ (inside 20–25¢). Cloud buys NO. Take when that bid ≥ 42¢. Stop when that ask ≤ 10¢. Flatten with 3 minutes left or window end. Always sells. If YES is 82¢ or NO is 18¢, it sits out — the band missed.
Start after / Until minuteDefault minutes 2–6. After Until, no new Spike fade buys. An open lot still exits.
Expensive min/maxDefault 75–80¢. The rich side must sit in this band.
Cheap min/maxDefault 20–25¢. The cheap side must sit in this band.
Take ask / Stop ask / Flatten leftDefault 42¢ bid / 10¢ ask / 3 minutes. Always dumps.
Lot contractsDefault 1. Size is Lot × live ask, not Auto $5.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On = fail closed on buy; can dump if the bid pile is thinner than you hold.

Isolation

  • Always the cheap side, not Auto lean
  • One lot per ticker. Never YES and NO together
  • Inside the enter window, Auto / Cash out / Gold fade sit that ticker out
  • Open Spike fade sits Home / Auto / Cash out / Gold fade / Last-minute / Step buy / Pair lock out
  • Last-minute owns new buys if it is in its window and Spike fade has no lot
  • Protect skips these rows

Limits

  • Cushions tab only supplies On/Off
  • Window cap 1
  • 5s grace after fill

Risk

  • A “spike” can keep going
  • Always dumps — you will not ride to $1
Both sides

Pair lock

Buy the lean as a runner. Later buy the other side only if the pair still locks a profit under $1. Hold both to settlement. This is the only path that may hold YES and NO on the same ticker. Admin On first. Empty chips = no buys. Default Off.

Example. Gold 3:00–3:15. At 3:02 YES is 52¢ (under Runner max 60¢). Cloud buys 1 YES. Hedge only if NO ≤ 43¢ (because 52¢ + NO + 5¢ Min lock must stay ≤ $1). At 3:08 NO is 18¢. Cloud buys 1 NO. Spent 70¢. Locked +30¢. Holds both to 3:15. If no NO by 3:12 (3 minutes left), it sells the YES at the bid.
Start after / Until minuteDefault 2 / 10. Until must be ≥ Start after. After Until, no new runner. An open runner may still hedge.
Runner max askDefault 60¢. Range 40–70¢.
Min lockDefault 5¢. Hedge only if runner fill + hedge ask ≤ $1 − Min lock. 50+50 sits out.
Flatten unmatchedDefault 3 minutes left. Dumps the runner only if the second leg is missing. A locked pair never flattens.
Lot contractsDefault 1. Hedge count matches the runner.
Skip thin bidDefault Off. On = fail closed on buys. Unmatched flatten can dump if the bid pile is thinner than you hold.

Isolation

  • Window cap 1 blocks the runner only. Hedge is extra
  • Open runner or pair: Home / Auto / Cash out / Gold fade / Last-minute / Step buy / Spike fade sit out
  • If Last-minute is in its buy window and Pair lock has no runner and no pair, Last-minute owns new buys
  • Spike fade and Step buy take first pick for new buys when they want the ticker
  • TWAP still owns BTC/ETH if On
  • Protect and Home Sell skip these rows

Limits

  • Does not use Auto $5 or Cushions $
  • No take/stop on a locked pair
  • Does not hedge another path’s lot

Risk

  • Naked runner from fill until flatten
  • If the Auto lean is wrong, flatten is the exit
  • Kalshi fees are not in Min lock — Min lock is a ¢ buffer

Skip thin bid, in one breath

Each Cloud path has its own checkbox. Default Off. Home Buy does not use it.

Cash out / Gold fadeSkip the buy. If you already hold, can sell. A book timeout does not skip or dump.
Spike fade / Pair lockSkip the buy if the book is unknown (fail closed). Can dump if the bid pile is thinner than you hold. Pair lock only dumps an unmatched runner — a locked pair holds.
TWAP / Last-minute / Step buySkip the buy only. Step buy stops still run. Unknown book size fails closed (no buy).
